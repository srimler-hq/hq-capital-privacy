Last updated: August 18, 2026

HQ Capital Inc. uses information obtained through eBay APIs to manage orders, product returns, and related internal business operations.

Information we process

We may process order identifiers, return details, shipment and tracking information, product information, and customer information supplied through eBay.

How we use information

We use this information to identify and process orders and returns, maintain business records, and provide customer service.

We do not sell eBay user information. Access is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers needed to operate our internal systems.

We retain information only as necessary for business, legal, accounting, and customer-service purposes. We process applicable eBay account-deletion notifications and remove associated information when required.
